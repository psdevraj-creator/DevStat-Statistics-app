How to download & install

1

Windows (.msi)

Click the Windows button above, get the .msi installer, and double-click it. Follow the single-screen setup wizard.

2

macOS (.dmg)

Click the macOS button above, open the .dmg, and drag DevStat into your Applications folder.

3

First launch

Open DevStat from your Start menu or Applications. A desktop-style window opens — click Data → Upload and choose your dataset.

4

Updates

DevStat checks for new versions on launch. When one is available, a prompt appears offering you to install it.
