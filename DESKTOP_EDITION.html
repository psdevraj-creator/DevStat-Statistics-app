FAQ

Common questions

Is my data really private? +

Yes. The statistics engine runs entirely on your computer. Your data is loaded, analysed and charted locally and never uploaded. Only your account and subscription details go to the server.

Do I need to register to use it? +

Yes — a quick free account is required, and it's the same account you'd use on the online app. This keeps your analysis, trial and licence in sync.

Why not an Electron/installer app? +

We replaced the heavyweight runtime with your own browser in app mode — smaller, faster and no extra software to install. It opens without an address bar, so it feels like a native app.

Does it need the internet every time? +

No. Once you're signed in, the analysis engine works with no connection at all. You only connect online to register or to check/activate your subscription.
